# SDCM

## Scope

SDCM is the Russian System of Differential Corrections and Monitoring associated with the GLONASS augmentation ecosystem.

This note is deliberately conservative. It records only the source-routed identity and development-context signals currently available in the knowledge base. It is not a declaration that SDCM has current aviation Safety-of-Life operational status, published LPV/APV procedures, certified receiver use, or regulator-approved airport/runway procedures.

## What can be said safely now

- SDCM is connected to the Russian GLONASS augmentation source family.
- Official GLONASS IAC / GLONASS OS PS material identifies SDCM as a GLONASS-based system certification context.
- ICAO-public historical material described SDCM as an SBAS development effort intended to provide integrity, refined ephemeris-time information, corrected measurement data, and constellation health status.
- Current operational aviation claims remain unverified in this KB.

## What cannot be said yet

Do not use this page to claim:

- current aviation service declaration;
- operational LPV/APV availability;
- Russian AIP procedure publication;
- exact service volume, coverage, availability, continuity, integrity, or time-to-alert commitments;
- aircraft/operator eligibility;
- equivalence with WAAS, EGNOS, MSAS, GAGAN, BDSBAS, KASS, or SouthPAN;
- support for ASEAN implementation decisions beyond a bounded comparator / research-target role.

## Research status

SDCM is now represented in the vault as a source-routed system page and child source note. Its status is intentionally lower-confidence than systems with extracted service-definition or provider material such as [[WAAS]], [[EGNOS]], [[GAGAN]], [[MSAS]], [[BDSBAS]], [[KASS]], and [[SouthPAN]].

This does not mean SDCM is unimportant. It means the current public evidence held by the KB is not yet sufficient for operational aviation claims.
