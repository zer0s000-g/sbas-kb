# GIPTA 2.0

## Definition status

GIPTA 2.0 is treated here as a program and implementation-learning layer for ASEAN GNSS/SBAS planning, not as an operational SBAS system.

## Working definition

In the local GIPTA document stack, GIPTA 2.0 is presented as a JRANSA-implemented, JAIF-supported ASEAN GNSS/SBAS capacity-building sequence involving symposia, site workshops, GNSS data-analysis equipment, SBAS simulation, and implementation-roadmap discussion.

## What this note adds without overlap

This note does not explain generic SBAS architecture. It captures the programmatic layer that connects ASEAN governance, testbed development, country participation, and implementation learning.

## Current GIPTA evidence pattern

- Dili, Timor-Leste hosted a GIPTA 2.0 GNSS Site Workshop for ASEAN on 14-15 April 2026.
- GIPTA source material frames SBAS implementation as staged: training, guideline/feasibility work, GNSS environment analysis, testbed/simulation, and capacity building.
- The documents repeatedly involve JRANSA, Japan/JAIF, ASEAN Secretariat or ASEAN transport context, ADB, and national aviation/geospatial stakeholders.
