# SBAS Non-Aviation Applications

## Definition status

This note captures non-aviation utilization as an implementation-economics topic. It does not replace aviation safety and certification analysis.

## Working definition

Non-aviation SBAS applications use augmentation-enabled positioning in sectors outside aviation, such as agriculture, logistics, maritime/port operations, smart-city/ITS, and drone or advanced-air-mobility support contexts.

## ASEAN relevance

GIPTA material frames multi-sector use as important because SBAS becomes more viable when its public-infrastructure value extends beyond aviation alone.

## Boundary

Sector use cases should be treated as value hypotheses until linked to national user communities, policy programs, or operational pilots.
