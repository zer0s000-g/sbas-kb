# 🛡️ Safety Terminology

## 📋 **Core Definitions**

- **Integrity Monitoring**: Continuous automated assessment of system performance and correctness
- **Protection Level (PL)**: Maximum probability of erroneous positioning exceeding specified thresholds
- **Alert Limit**: Boundary that, when exceeded, triggers a warning to the user
- **Detection Time**: Time between fault occurrence and warning issuance
- **Availability**: Percentage of time system is operational and providing valid information
