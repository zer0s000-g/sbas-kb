# ASEAN SBAS Testbed to Operational Service

## Scope status
This note explains the transition problem between testbed/simulation work and operational service. It does not claim that the transition is complete.

## Core distinction
A testbed can support feasibility, training, data accumulation, ionospheric monitoring, and SBAS message simulation. Operational SBAS service requires a different level of safety assurance, governance, procedure approval, spectrum protection, continuity commitment, and institutional ownership.

## GIPTA-supported transition steps
- GNSS analyzer deployment and data sharing
- low-latitude ionospheric environment survey
- SBAS message simulation
- guideline / feasibility / master-plan work
- capacity building for operators, policymakers, and engineers

## Remaining operational gaps
- service provider and liability model
- regional or national certification pathway
- validated ionospheric threat model for ASEAN conditions
- RFI monitoring and response model
- funding and user-fee arrangement
- aircraft equipage and procedure rollout strategy
